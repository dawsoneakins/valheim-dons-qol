# Don's QOL v0.5.3 release notes

## [0.5.3] - 2026-11-13

Fixes and two new features. Everyone playing together needs the same version, so update as a group. Your settings, chest categories, favourites and saves carry over.

### New

- **Cooking stations collect their own food.** Cooking stations, iron cooking stations and ovens get a "Cooked food" button in the feed panel that cycles Off, To chests and Drop. A station you keep fed sends finished food to your Cooked Food chests unless you change it. Charred food is left alone. Fermenters aren't covered yet.
- **The compendium is now a journey.** It opens on a Home page with found counts per land, recent discoveries, what your base can make and a few hints. Item pages show how many you have in the base and can make, and can light up the chests holding them. Creature pages rank your weapons against them. A new "Not yet found" tab shows silhouettes for lands you've visited, with up to three hints each, and you can track a hint below the minimap. Spoiler options are per character.

### Fixed

- **"Inventory full" with free slots.** Worn gear no longer takes up hidden space, so auto-pickup, crafting and fishing see every free slot.
- **Pocket upgrades and worn gear.** Buying Haldor's extra inventory rows no longer clashes with worn gear. Graves are sized to fit what's in them.
- **Base management move.** Moving a base now reports its result in the Bases window, and each move is logged.
